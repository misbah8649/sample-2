Seven Wonders of the World

Wonder Image Description
Taj Mahal (India) The Taj Mahal is a beautiful white marble mausoleum built by Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal.
Great Wall of China (China) The Great Wall of China is one of the longest walls in the world, built to protect China from invasions.
Petra (Jordan) Petra is an ancient city famous for its rock-cut architecture and historical significance.
Christ the Redeemer (Brazil) Christ the Redeemer is a giant statue of Jesus Christ located on Corcovado Mountain in Rio de Janeiro.
Machu Picchu (Peru) Machu Picchu is an ancient Incan city located high in the Andes Mountains.
Chichen Itza (Mexico) Chichen Itza is a famous Mayan archaeological site known for the El Castillo pyramid.
Colosseum (Italy) The Colosseum is an ancient Roman amphitheater that hosted gladiator contests and public events.
